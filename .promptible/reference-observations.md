## Composition
openmontage-hook contact sheet (3 panels, light warm-gray world): left panel opens tight on a crowded tab strip (CapCut / Suno tabs, gray text lines) — dense, edge-cropped, already in motion. Center panel pulls back to reveal a floating constellation of ~10 small white work surfaces (script draft, captions, voiceover, timeline, soundtrack, export 47%, tile grid) orbiting a dark terminal core. Right panel settles on payoff: dark terminal ("turn this repo into my video studio / OpenMontage loaded") above a pastel timeline and 37% progress bar, plus small waveform/signal cards. Few large focal objects at payoff; generous negative space; hierarchy tight-fragment → system → consequence. No centered title slide.

## Camera
Single shared world, hold-travel-hold grammar. Opens tight and cropped (fragment fills frame), holds while fragments accumulate, then smooth pullback/travel to reveal the full constellation and spatial relationships, then settles into a stable centered payoff hold. Moves are motivated (reveal context, then consequence), ~14-24f travels with cubic in-out, repeated keys for holds. No whip-pans, no empty travel, no slow-zoom-only. Action completes during holds; destinations are readable at phone scale. End hold is clean for cutting.

## Identity
Authentic tool surfaces carry identity: real product names (CapCut, Suno, Descript, ElevenLabs, Premiere, Pexels), real UI structures (tab strips, timelines, track lists, export progress), real terminal command + output. Flat white paper cards with thin borders and soft neutral shadows on warm gray; dark terminal core as contrast anchor; tiny red close-dots and muted color chips as the only accents. Type is small structural UI labels, never narrated sentences. Specific on first pass — could only be a fragmented-toolchain story.

## Research match
TURYA beat maps 1:1 to hook mechanism: five verified site activities (Hackathons & Game Jams, Content & Design, Course Creation, Teaching & Mentorship, Learn by Building / Ship) are the scattered fragments; TURYA wordmark + "Go Beyond. Make Something." + "ecosystem for young builders" is the authentic center they collapse toward. Translation: light paper cards become dark-world paper/ink chips with gold rule (brand tokens ink #060606, paper #f2f0e8, gold #c9a24b); terminal core becomes TURYA identity lockup; export progress becomes tagline reveal. Camera grammar, scale (~2x viewport world), restraint, and causal collapse transfer directly. No dashboard invention — fragments are short structural labels from site copy.

## Anti-pattern
What we will not copy or build: no neon/glow/bloom/colored shadows/gradient washes/glassmorphism (reference has none — flat cards, neutral shadows only). No receipt/dossier/stamp/barcode/pricing-card fallback, no disconnected card grid (reference cards share one world with spatial logic). No fake metrics beyond verified copy (no invented counts, prices, engagement). No rows-appearing-plus-slow-zoom (reference moves are spatial pullbacks with causal collapse). No redundant narration headlines. Dark clean-dark surface is user-chosen and brand-authentic (Turya site is ink-based), rendered as neutral charcoal-ink with flat accents, never illuminated panels.
