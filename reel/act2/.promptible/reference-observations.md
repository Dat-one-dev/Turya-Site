## Composition
openmontage-hook contact sheet (inspected for the sting): tight fragment open → constellation around a dark core → payoff as consequence. Act 2 uses the same grammar with a practice wall instead of floating cards: five full-width rows sharing dividers (number / title / short text, echoing the site's activity-list), one gold progress rule, "ECOSYSTEM IN ACTION" section line as payoff. Rows are large and few; the wall fills the middle 75% safe zone. The Act 1 lockup opens the act small and recedes (match cut), so the reel reads as one continuous world rather than disconnected scenes.

## Camera
Match-cut travel from the lockup to the wall, then hold while rows stagger in (6f offsets) with the gold rule filling as their consequence, slow pullback to reveal the full wall + section line, then a 100-frame stable end hold for the Act 3 handoff. Cubic in-out, holds created by repeated keys. One primary action per beat: travel, accumulation, reveal, settle. No empty travel; camera moves to the next action and the action completes during the hold.

## Identity
Row content is verbatim app/page.tsx activities (names + short phrases like "by doing, not just watching" and "Builders teaching builders"). Turya tokens throughout (ink/paper/fog, one gold rule as the single hero element), fog uppercase eyebrows, starfield grain. No logos to redraw, no third-party surfaces, no invented statuses — rows ARE the site's activity list, simplified for 9:16 legibility.

## Research match
Every row traces to the activities array (numbers, titles, descriptions) and the "Ecosystem in action" section copy. The gold progress rule filling with row completions is the hook mechanism's "state change as consequence" applied to verified content. No outcomes are claimed (no shipped counts, no results) — completion here means the row checked in, nothing more.

## Anti-pattern
No neon/glow/gradient/glass; flat surfaces, neutral shadows. The wall shares dividers and one rule — never floating disconnected cards. No fake metrics, ranks, or engagement. No slow-zoom-only camera. No narration headlines repeating VO; the section line is structural site copy. Dark is user-chosen, rendered flat.
