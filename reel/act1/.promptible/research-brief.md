10 seconds, 9:16 (1080x1920), 30fps, 300 frames. One connected ink world (2x viewport). Shared camera rig (fx/fy/z, cubic in-out, hold-travel-hold): 0-60f open tight on fragment 01 already drifting; 60-130f hold as five chips stagger in (4f offsets); 130-180f pullback revealing constellation + connector rules + anchor ring; 180-250f fragments collapse into center, TURYA springs in, gold rule wipes, tagline word-reveals; 250-300f stable hold on lockup for the Act 2 match cut.
